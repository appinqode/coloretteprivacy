Colorette

Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy describes how informations and data is handled by Colorette app ("the App").

Last updated: August 1 2026.

1. Introduction

Colorette is the smart and simple macOS utility to create stunning color palettes, extract colors from images, create design systems, build tints and shades, design mesh gradients, and export ready-to-use CSV, CSS, Xcode .colorset assets and SwiftUI code.

2. Information Collection

Colorette is designed with privacy in mind. The App does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal or technical information.

3. Data Storage

Colorette does not maintain remote servers. No user data is stored externally. Any app preferences or settings are stored locally on the user device using standard macOS system mechanisms.

4. Third - Party Services

Colorette does not use third - party analytics, advertising frameworks, tracking technologies, or external data processing services.

5. Internet Connectivity

Colorette does not require an internet connection to perform its core functionality.

6. Data Sharing

Because Colorette does not collect personal data, no information is shared.

7. Security

Since all operations occur locally on the user device, all information remains under the user control.

8. Children's Privacy

Colorette does not collect any information from childrens or users of any age.

9. Changes to this Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any modification may be made without the user's prior or subsequent consent or notice. Changes will be reflected by updating this webpage.
